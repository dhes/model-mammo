Purpose

Preventive Health Companion reviews your health information to display preventive care recommendations based on guidelines from the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF). The app is designed to help you have informed conversations with your healthcare provider about preventive care services that may be appropriate for you.

Not Medical Advice

The information provided by this app is for educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or preventive care services.

Data Access

This app accesses your health information in read-only mode to evaluate which preventive care guidelines may apply to you. We do not store, transmit, or share your health information outside of your current session. Your data remains protected by your healthcare provider's systems and is accessed only with your explicit authorization.

Guideline Limitations

Preventive care recommendations are based on USPSTF guidelines, which provide general guidance for the average patient. Your individual circumstances, preferences, and health history may warrant different recommendations. The absence of a recommendation does not mean a service is inappropriate for you, and the presence of a recommendation does not mean it is required.

This app is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of the information displayed. Clinical guidelines are updated periodically, and there may be a delay between guideline changes and app updates.
